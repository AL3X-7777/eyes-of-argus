Shared list

Subscribe to a list someone maintains: any file exported from this extension, hosted anywhere (for example a GitHub repo). It updates twice a day when you're online. Offline, the last copy is used. Your own marks always win.

Backup & sharing

Export your marks to share them or keep a backup. Restore backup is for a file you exported yourself: it restores it exactly. Import list is for someone else's file: it only adds "yes" marks where you have no opinion, and isn't sent as your votes.

Help

  1. Right-click a channel or account name, a video or post, a search result, or an empty spot on a channel or profile page. Works on YouTube, X, Reddit, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch, Facebook, and on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo results (a result marks its whole website).
  2. Choose The Eyes of Argus → Mark as AI or Mark as private-equity owned. A channel can have both.
  3. Marked channels get a badge everywhere they appear. Use Clear to remove a mark.
  4. If you think a community mark is wrong, choose Not AI or Not PE-owned. That hides it for you and counts as a vote against it.

Nothing shows up? Reload tabs that were open before the extension was installed. Some places, like parts of the watch-page sidebar, show the channel name without a link. Right-click the video's thumbnail or open the video instead.

Privacy: your marks never leave your browser unless you export them. With community sync on, only your votes (channel, flag, yes/no) and an anonymous ID are sent to the sync server you set. Everyone downloads the same full list, so the server never learns which videos you watch.
